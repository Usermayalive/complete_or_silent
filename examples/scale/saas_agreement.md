# SaaS Subscription Agreement

## 1. Definitions

"Subscription Term" means the initial term of twelve months and any renewal term.

"Service Levels" means the availability commitments set out in Section 5.

"Customer Data" means all data uploaded to the Platform by the Customer or its users.

"Affiliate" means any entity that controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a party.

## 2. Subscription

The Provider grants the Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to use the Platform during the Subscription Term. The Customer may permit its Affiliates to use the Platform.

The Customer shall not: (a) resell or sublicense the Platform; (b) reverse engineer the Platform; or (c) use the Platform to build a competing product.

## 3. Fees

| Plan | Monthly fee | Users |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 1,000 USD | 50 |
| Enterprise | 4,000 USD | Unlimited |

Fees are invoiced annually in advance and are payable within thirty days of the invoice date.

The Provider may increase fees on renewal by no more than five percent, subject to Section 9.2.

## 4. Data Protection

The Provider shall process Customer Data only on the Customer's documented instructions.

Customer Data is stored in the European Union. The Provider shall notify the Customer of any personal data breach within seventy-two hours.

## 5. Service Levels

The Provider shall make the Platform available 99.9 percent of the time in each calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance.

If availability falls below the Service Levels, the Customer is entitled to the service credits in Section 6. The Provider shall respond to critical incidents within four hours.

## 6. Service Credits

The Customer receives a credit of ten percent of the monthly fee for each full percentage point below the Service Levels, up to fifty percent of the monthly fee. Service credits are the Customer's sole remedy for availability failures.

## 7. Support

Support is available by email during business hours. Enterprise customers receive twenty-four hour telephone support.

The Provider shall respond to critical incidents within one hour.

## 8. Limitation of Liability

Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential losses. Each party's total liability is limited to the fees paid in the twelve months before the claim.

The limitation in this Section 8 does not apply to breaches of Section 4.

## 9. Term and Renewal

### 9.1 Renewal

The Subscription Term renews automatically for successive twelve month periods unless either party gives sixty days' notice of non-renewal.

### 9.2 Price Changes

The Provider must give ninety days' written notice of any fee increase. Fees may not increase during the initial Subscription Term.

## 10. Termination

The Customer may terminate for convenience on thirty days' notice. No refund is payable on termination for convenience.

On termination, the Provider shall make Customer Data available for export for thirty days and then delete it.

The Provider may suspend access immediately if the Customer breaches Section 2(c).
