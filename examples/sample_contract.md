# Master Services Agreement

## 1. Definitions

"Losses" means all damages, liabilities, costs and reasonable legal fees incurred by a party.

"Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by one party to the other, whether oral or written.

"Misconduct" means fraud, gross negligence or wilful breach of this Agreement.

## 2. Services

The Supplier shall provide the services described in each Statement of Work. Each Statement of Work forms part of this Agreement.

The Customer shall pay all undisputed invoices within thirty days of receipt.

## 3. Confidentiality

Each party shall keep the other party's Confidential Information secret and use it only to perform this Agreement.

These obligations survive termination for a period of five years.

## 4. Liability

### 4.1 Cap on Liability

Except as set out in Section 4.2, each party's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the fees paid in the twelve months before the claim.

### 4.2 Exclusions from the Cap

The cap in Section 4.1 does not apply to Losses arising from Misconduct or from a breach of Section 3.

## 5. Indemnity

The Supplier shall indemnify the Customer against all Losses arising from the Supplier's Misconduct. The indemnity does not cover Losses caused by the Customer's own instructions.

Claims under this indemnity are subject to the exclusions in Section 4.2.

## 6. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement on ninety days' written notice. Either party may terminate immediately if the other commits a material breach that is not remedied within thirty days.

On termination, the Customer shall pay for all services performed up to the termination date.
